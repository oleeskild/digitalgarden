# Reunion Feedback Summary

## 1. Overall Support for Another Reunion
Most respondents enthusiastically support having another reunion. There were only two exceptions:
*   **Colin Glennie** stated that this most recent event would be his last reunion.
*   **Richard Harden** felt that they should perhaps not have another one to avoid an anti-climax, and that they shouldn't go on too long.

## 2. Preferred Interval
For those who want another reunion, the vast majority agree that two years (or "every other year") is the ideal interval:
*   **2 Years:** John Wright, Peter Newbery, David Talbot, Campbell de Burgh, Jim Reed, Geoff Cluett, John Evans, and Bill Shaw.
*   **1 Year:** Bryan (Gale) suggested one year so as not to "tempt fate too much!"
*   **Other:** David Crothers suggested meeting "in a few years' time", while Peter Newbery explicitly noted that 1 year is too soon and 5 years is too long.

## 3. Venue & Format (Wylder vs. Alternative)
*   **Returning to Wylder:** There is widespread support for returning to Wylder, which was highly praised (John W, Peter N, David T, Campbell, Jim, Gale, John E, Bill).
    *   *Campbell de Burgh* would prefer a two-day event if returning.
    *   *John Wright* noted that while Wylder is excellent, the access lanes lack proper signposting.
*   **Alternative Formats:** Some attendees suggested considering a different format to prevent the next reunion from feeling like a less memorable repeat (Greg Cumming, Geoff Cluett).
    *   *Geoff Cluett* suggested a 3-day river boat cruise.
    *   *Peter Newbery* anticipated that others might prefer a less ambitious format next time.

## 4. Additional Comments
*   **The Silver Mug:** *Peter Newbery* mentioned the need for another event to pass on Geoff's silver sailing mug (won by his dad in 1959) to a new competition winner.
*   **Offers of Help:** *Bill Shaw* offered to assist the organizers (the "triumvirate") with the next event. *Greg Cumming* playfully stated that he shouldn't be looked to for taking over the organization.
*   **Attendance Scope:** *Peter Newbery* suggested that restricting the reunion specifically to Engineers is the right approach.
*   **Physical Capability Caveats:** Several attendees, including David Crothers, Geoff Cluett, and Peter Newbery, noted that attendance in the future will depend heavily on everyone remaining physically capable.
