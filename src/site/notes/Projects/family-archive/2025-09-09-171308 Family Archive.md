The notes in this collection explore the concept of a private, online space to be used as a family archive and for family communication. The motivations for this and the purposes that flow from these are  [[Projects/family-archive/2025-09-09-172517 Purpose - family archive\|described here]]. The design concept is outlined below.

![Pasted image 20250909172319.png](/img/user/Attachments/images/Pasted%20image%2020250909172319.png)

The family archive uses the metaphor of the building in which it might be held if it were a physical archive.

Current assumptions are (1) that members of the extended family will form an [[Projects/family-archive/2025-09-09-173158 The family archive as an unincorporated association – family archive\|unincorporated association]] and (2) that the association will fund a small company to handle contracts for the technical infrastructure; each of these has an 'office' within the building.  

The 'meeting room' will contain a social network similar to the original Facebook concept. The 'family tree' room will be a research centre with links out to a genealogy site; a further assumption is that this will be WikiTree which, at first sight, looks tailor-made for our purpose.
